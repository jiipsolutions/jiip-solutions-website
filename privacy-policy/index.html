Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you interact with our website and services.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

JIIP Solutions is a UAE-based digital marketing and content creation business. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be collected, used, shared and protected when you visit www.jiipsolutions.com or communicate with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact us directly, we may collect information including:

  • Your name and business name.
  • Your email address and phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Your business sector and service requirements.
  • Information you provide about your business goals.
  • Other information you voluntarily share with us.

Our hosting and third-party service providers may also process technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and website request information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and service requests.
  • Understand your business requirements.
  • Prepare relevant proposals and quotations.
  • Communicate about requested or agreed services.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Meet applicable legal and business obligations.

Where required, we obtain consent or rely on another lawful basis permitted by applicable data protection laws.

3. Contact Forms and Third-Party Services

Our website uses Web3Forms to process and deliver contact form submissions. Information submitted through our enquiry form is transmitted through this service.

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages and may load external resources from third-party providers. These services may process technical information required to deliver website functionality.

If you contact us through WhatsApp, email or social media platforms, your information may also be processed according to the privacy policies of those providers.

4. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website and external service providers may use technical resources necessary for website delivery and functionality.

If we introduce additional analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential tracking technologies, we will update this policy and implement appropriate consent mechanisms where required.

5. Information Sharing

Personal information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to operate our website, respond to enquiries or deliver agreed services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect legitimate legal rights.

6. International Data Processing

Some third-party service providers may process or store information outside the United Arab Emirates. Where applicable, international transfers must be handled in accordance with relevant data protection requirements.

7. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including communication, service delivery and applicable legal obligations.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable data protection laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant authority.

You may contact us using the details below to submit a privacy-related request.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our website enquiry forms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website functionality or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be available on this page with its updated revision date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us.
